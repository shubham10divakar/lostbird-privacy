Privacy Policy — Lost Bird

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Lost Bird is developed by Subham Divakar. This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you play Lost Bird and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

If you sign in with Google

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Google Account ID (used as a unique identifier)

If you play as Guest

  • An anonymous unique identifier generated by Firebase (no name or email collected)

Game data (all users)

  • Game progress (levels completed, high scores, star ratings)
  • Selected bird
  • Infinite mode leaderboard score

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save your game progress and sync it across devices
  • To display your name and score on the global leaderboard
  • To identify your account when you return to the game

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than running the game.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore). Firebase is a Google service subject to Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Game progress is also saved locally on your device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your display name and high score are visible to other players on the public leaderboard if you participate in Infinite Mode.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists in the game. You can request deletion by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Lost Bird is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
